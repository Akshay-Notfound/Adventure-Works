### 🌟 System Overview

**Adventure Works Cycles BI Analytics** delivers comprehensive business intelligence across global multinational manufacturing and distribution operations. Spanning operations in North America, Europe, and Asia, it tracks end-to-end sales pipelines, regional gross margins, and manufacturing capacity utilization.

---

### 🔬 Operational Architecture & Data Pipeline Flow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[AdventureWorks OLTP Database] --> B[ETL Extraction & Star Schema Modeling]
    B --> C[FactSales & Dimension Tables: Date, Territory, Product]
    C --> D[Advanced DAX & SQL Measures: Margin%, YoY Growth]
    D --> E[Multi-Page Executive Tableau Storyboards]
    E --> F[Territory Quota & Profitability Optimization]
```

---

### 🚀 Key Capabilities & Features

- **🚴 Multi-Regional Sales Insights:** Tracks regional distributor sales across continents with exchange rate normalization.
- **📈 YoY & MoM Growth Modeling:** Calculates compounded annual growth rates and territory quota achievements.
- **📦 Product Profitability Matrix:** Identifies high-margin bicycle components vs loss-leader accessories.
- **🌟 Star Schema Architecture:** High-performance dimensional design minimizing query execution latencies.
